# Business Requirements

## Objective

Analyze e-commerce business performance using sales data and provide actionable insights.

## Key Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. Which category generates the highest revenue?
3. Which states contribute the most revenue?
4. Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
5. What are monthly sales trends?
6. What percentage of orders are delivered, cancelled, returned, or pending?
7. Which products perform best?

## Expected Outcome

Develop an interactive dashboard that allows stakeholders to quickly monitor business performance.
