# Business Insights

## Revenue Insights

- Fashion generated the highest revenue (₹3.2M).
- Maharashtra was the highest revenue-generating state (₹2.05M).
- Mobile App contributed approximately 75% of total sales.

## Customer & Order Insights

- Total Orders: 1,100
- Delivered Orders: 554
- Average Order Value: ₹12.2K

## Returns & Cancellations

- Return and Cancellation Rate: 32.45%
- Books contributed the highest return/cancellation value.
- COD orders represented 23% of all returned orders.

## Seasonal Insights

- July generated the highest monthly revenue.
- Sales remained relatively strong between June and October.

## Recommendations

- Encourage prepaid transactions.
- Reduce return rates through quality checks.
- Expand marketing in Maharashtra and West Bengal.
- Increase investment in Fashion category products.
